5. Reliability of alerts

The extension does its best to warn you in time, but an alert may be late, incomplete or missed, in particular if:

  • the browser is closed, or the computer is asleep or offline (alerts only work while the browser is open, even minimized);
  • notifications are blocked by the operating system (“Do Not Disturb” mode, Windows / macOS settings);
  • data from third-party providers is late, wrong, changed or unavailable (rescheduled times, an announcement added or canceled, a figure published late);
  • Investing.com rejects or blocks requests (anti-bot protection): the extension then switches to Forex Factory, which does not provide the actual figure and only covers the current week;
  • a provider changes the format of its data, which may make the extension temporarily inoperative.

Do not rely exclusively on the extension for important decisions: check the calendar against an official source.
