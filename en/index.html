Features

Everything you need to be ready before the release

Built for forex, index and commodity traders who are tired of getting caught off guard by release-day volatility. Everything is included, for free.

Economic calendar

Releases over a rolling 8-day window (Investing.com, with Forex Factory as an automatic fallback or by choice): time in your time zone, currency, impact, forecast and previous value.

Alert before every release

System notification, an always-on-top pop-up window with a live countdown, and a sound. Impossible to miss, even when you're deep in a chart.

Impact & currency filters

Follow only what matters: High, Medium or Low impact, or bank holidays, and any of the 9 currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CHF, CAD, AUD, NZD, CNY).

Multiple lead times

Get alerted 60, 30, 15, 10 or 5 minutes before, or right on time — even several times for the same release. The icon badge shows the countdown.

Quiet hours

No alerts at night or during your downtime. Asian-session releases won't wake you up anymore.

Mute per release

A bell next to every release: silence the alert for one specific event without touching your settings.

Result as soon as it's out

One minute after each release you follow, a notification shows the actual figure against the forecast: ▲ better or ▼ worse than expected for the currency. You can see at a glance which way the market is likely to react.

How it works

Ready in under two minutes

  1. 1

    Install the extension

    Add EcoQuant to Chrome, Edge or Brave (see installation), then pin the icon to the toolbar.

  2. 2

Manual installation instructions

  1. 1

    Download the ZIP file

    "Download ZIP" button above (archive EcoQuant.zip).
  2. 2

    Extract the ZIP contents to a folder

    Right-click → Extract All. Keep the folder somewhere permanent: don't delete it after installing.
  3. 3

    Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions/

    Edge: edge://extensions · Brave: brave://extensions. For security reasons, these addresses can't be opened from a link: paste it into the address bar.
  4. 4

    Turn on "Developer mode" in the top right corner

    On the left in Edge.
  5. 5

    Click "Load unpacked" and select the extension folder (inside the extracted EcoQuant folder)

    It's the one that contains manifest.json. The settings page opens automatically.
  6. 6

    Pin the 🧩 icon to see the countdown

    Puzzle icon → pin: the badge shows the countdown to the next release.

When installed this way, the extension doesn't update itself: for a new version, replace the folder's contents, then click ⟳ on the extensions page. Chrome may also show a warning about developer-mode extensions at startup: that's normal.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free? Do I need to create an account?

Yes, 100% free and no account needed: every feature (alerts, 9 currencies, all impact levels, multiple lead times, result after release, position size calculator) is included. No ads, no subscription, no tracking. The source code is open, under the MIT license.

Do I need to keep the browser open?

Yes. Alerts are triggered by the extension, so Chrome (or Edge / Brave) has to be open, even if it's minimized to the taskbar. If the browser is closed or the computer is asleep, no alert can be shown.

I'm not getting notifications on Windows. What should I do?

Open Settings → System → Notifications, make sure notifications are turned on for Google Chrome (or Edge / Brave) and that Do not disturb / Focus assist is turned off.

On macOS: System Settings → Notifications → Google Chrome → Allow notifications. Either way, the Fire a test alert button in Settings lets you check that everything works. The pop-up window, for its part, shows up even if system notifications are blocked.

Where does the data come from? In what time zone are releases shown?

By default, the calendar comes from Investing.com (rolling 8 days, with the actual figure after release). You can also pick Forex Factory in Settings: a very reliable feed, but limited to the current week and without actual figures.

Investing.com sometimes rejects requests (anti-bot protection). The extension then switches automatically to Forex Factory (orange message in the popup) and retries Investing.com a little later. To get it unblocked faster: open investing.com once in your browser, then click ⟳ in the popup.

Times are converted to your computer's time zone: no math needed, even when you travel.

Why don't I see the result after a release?

The result (actual figure vs forecast) is only available with the Investing.com source. It arrives about one minute after the release; if the figure is late, the extension retries every minute for a few minutes. Also make sure the "Result after release" option is turned on in Settings.

How does the position size calculator work?

Open the popup and go to the Position size calculator tab. Exchange rates (ECB) are fetched automatically and can be entered manually under "Advanced". For indices and metals, the value of a point depends on your broker: check it and adjust it under "Advanced". The result is indicative.

What happens to my data?

It stays in your browser. The extension has no server and sends nothing to its publisher. Details in the privacy policy.

I found a bug or have an idea. How can I let you know?

Open an issue on GitHub (Issues) describing what's happening (browser, selected source, screenshot if possible), or email pinodamien@gmail.com. See also the Contact page.

Is this investment advice?

No. EcoQuant is an information and organization tool. It does not recommend buying or selling anything. Trading involves a high risk of losing capital. See the terms of use.
