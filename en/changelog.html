Version 1.3.0 Current version

November 6, 2026 · first release on the Chrome Web Store

  • Economic calendar over 8 days (Investing.com, with automatic fallback to Forex Factory when needed), shown in your own time zone.
  • Alerts before every release: 60, 30, 15, 10 or 5 minutes before, or right on time. Several lead times can be combined.
  • Filters by impact level (high, medium, low, bank holidays) and by currency (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CHF, CAD, AUD, NZD, CNY).
  • Three ways to be alerted: system notification, pop-up window with countdown, sound. Each one can be turned on separately.
  • Actual figure on release: the actual number vs the forecast, ▲ better or ▼ worse than expected for the currency.
  • Position size calculator: capital, risk in % or amount, stop loss and take profit → lots, amount at risk, pip value and R:R ratio (forex, gold, silver, indices, Bitcoin).
  • Comfort: quiet hours, a bell to mute a single release, countdown on the extension icon.
  • 100% free, no account and no data collected.

The extension interface is currently in French.
